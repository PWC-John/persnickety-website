Common Questions

What homeowners want to know.

Do you clean both the inside and outside of windows?

Yes. We offer complete interior and exterior cleaning, as well as exterior-only service when that is the better fit for your home.

Do you clean screens, sills and tracks?

Screens, sills and tracks can be included. We confirm the exact scope during the estimate so there are no surprises.

Can you clean high or hard-to-reach windows?

Yes. We regularly handle ladder-access windows, tall interior glass, large panes and other difficult residential glass after evaluating safe access.

What about storm windows and French panes?

We clean traditional storms, multi-surface systems and divided-light French panes. These windows take more time, so their condition and configuration are considered in the estimate.

How do I get an estimate?

Call or text 330-353-8326 with your address. Photos are particularly helpful for large homes, storms, French panes and difficult windows.
