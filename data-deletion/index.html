Data Deletion Instructions

You may request deletion of personal information associated with Persnickety Window Cleaning, including information received through Meta lead forms and stored in the Persnickety HOWARD business application.

How to request deletion
  1. Email john.winter@persnicketywindowcleaning.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include your name and the phone number or email address you used when contacting Persnickety so we can identify the correct record.
  3. If your request relates specifically to a Facebook or Instagram lead form, mention that in your email.

What we will do

After we can reasonably identify the applicable record, we will delete or anonymize personal information that we are not required to retain. This may include lead-form details, contact information, notes, message history, or other customer information stored in our business systems.

Information we may retain

Some records may need to be retained when reasonably necessary for accounting, tax, legal, payment, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate business recordkeeping purposes. When possible, information not needed for those purposes will be deleted or minimized.

Confirmation

We will respond to the contact information provided with the request when the deletion review has been completed or if we need additional information to identify the correct record.

Meta-connected data

Persnickety HOWARD may receive lead information submitted through Facebook or Instagram forms. A deletion request made through the instructions above will include that data when it can be matched to your Persnickety record. You may also use privacy and account controls provided directly by Meta for information Meta maintains independently.

Questions

Questions about deletion requests may be sent to john.winter@persnicketywindowcleaning.com.
