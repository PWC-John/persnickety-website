What to Include

Better details produce a better first answer.

You do not need a perfect window count. The information below is enough to understand the property and decide whether photos, a walkthrough or a direct quote is the right next step.

For a home

  • Property address
  • Interior, exterior or both
  • Approximate home size or window count
  • Storms, screens, French panes or skylights
  • Photos of the exterior and difficult windows
  • Preferred timeframe

For a business

  • Business and property address
  • One-time or recurring service
  • Interior, exterior or both
  • Storefront, office, dealership or facility
  • Access hours and site considerations
  • Photos, plans or scope documents

Estimate Questions

Before you reach out.

What should I send for an estimate?

Send the property address, whether the job is residential or commercial, whether you want interior, exterior or both, your preferred timeframe and photos when possible.

Can I text photos?

Yes. Photos of the exterior and any storms, French panes, skylights or difficult-access glass are especially helpful.

Can a business request an estimate by email?

Yes. Email john.winter@persnicketywindowcleaning.com with the property address, scope, desired frequency, access considerations and any useful attachments.

Does submitting a request automatically book the work?

No. You are contacting John directly. The scope and next step are reviewed before anything is scheduled.
