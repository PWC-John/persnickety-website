Privacy Policy

How Persnickety Window Cleaning handles information from this website and customer communications.

Effective August 24, 2026

Overview

Persnickety Window Cleaning respects your privacy. This policy explains the information we may receive when you visit persnicketywindowcleaning.com or contact us, and how we use that information.

Information you provide

When you call, text or email us, you may provide information such as your name, phone number, email address, service address, photos, property details and the contents of your message. We use this information to respond to questions, prepare estimates, schedule and perform services, communicate about work and maintain ordinary business records.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, referring source, interactions and cookie or similar identifiers. This information helps us improve the website and understand which services people are looking for.

Cookies and browser choices

Analytics tools may use cookies or similar technologies. You can limit or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking analytics cookies should not prevent you from viewing the website, but some measurements may be less accurate.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support the website and business, such as website hosting, analytics, email, phone or messaging providers. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety or property.

Data retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide service, maintain business and tax records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no website, email or messaging system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have provided, request a correction or ask us to delete information when it is not required for ongoing service, recordkeeping or legal obligations.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for people seeking window cleaning services and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or business practices change. The effective date above shows when this version was last updated.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to john.winter@persnicketywindowcleaning.com or discussed by calling 330-353-8326.

This policy is written to describe the current website and ordinary customer communications. It is general business information and is not legal advice.
