Information we collect

How we use information

We use this information to respond to inquiries, prepare estimates, schedule and perform services, communicate about appointments, maintain customer and job records, follow up on estimates or rebooking opportunities, improve our operations, and understand which marketing sources produce customer inquiries and completed work.

Persnickety HOWARD

Persnickety HOWARD is our internal business application. It may receive customer or lead information from our website, Meta lead forms, messaging providers, and information we enter ourselves. HOWARD is used to organize customer records, estimates, jobs, communications, follow-ups, and business reporting. Customer messages are not automatically sent solely because information enters HOWARD; owner review is used for customer-facing messaging workflows where applicable.

Service providers

We may use service providers to host our website and business systems, process communications, receive lead information, provide analytics, and operate related business technology. These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services to us and according to their own applicable terms and privacy commitments.

Data retention and security

We retain business and customer information for as long as reasonably needed for customer service, scheduling, accounting, legal, operational, and recordkeeping purposes. We use reasonable safeguards intended to protect information, but no internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete personal information we maintain about you, subject to records we may need to retain for legitimate business, tax, legal, or accounting purposes.
