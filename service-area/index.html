Service Area Questions

Before we put you on the route.

What areas does Persnickety Window Cleaning serve?

We are based in Cuyahoga Falls and regularly serve Cuyahoga Falls, Silver Lake, Bath, Stow, Tallmadge, Akron, Macedonia, Richfield, Copley, Coventry Township, Peninsula, Munroe Falls, Fairlawn, Portage Lakes, Norton, Kent and Hudson.

Do you travel outside the listed service area?

Some projects outside the regular area may make sense based on the location, job size and schedule. Text the property address to 330-353-8326 and we will give you a direct answer.

Do you serve both homes and businesses?

Yes. We provide residential, commercial and specialty window cleaning throughout our Northeast Ohio service area.

How do I confirm whether you serve my address?

Call or text 330-353-8326 with the property address. Photos and a short description of the windows can help us give you the right next step.
