Commercial FAQs

What businesses ask before scheduling.

Do you offer recurring commercial window cleaning?

Yes. Recurring maintenance and one-time service are both available. The right interval depends on traffic, visibility, weather exposure and the type of property.

Can you clean both interior and exterior glass?

Yes. Interior, exterior or complete service can be quoted after reviewing the building, access and requested scope.

Will the work disrupt customers or employees?

We plan around entrances, customer areas and normal operations whenever practical. Scheduling expectations are confirmed before work begins.

Do you provide commercial invoicing?

Yes. The scope and invoicing details are confirmed when the work is scheduled.

How do I get an estimate?

Call or text 330-353-8326 with the property address, business type and desired service. Photos help with large buildings, tall glass and unusual access.
