Specialty Services

The extra details are the whole point.

Some windows cannot be priced or cleaned like standard glass. We account for the construction, number of surfaces, height, access and care each area requires.

01

Storm Windows

Traditional storms and multi-surface window systems are handled one section and one surface at a time.

02

Screens

Screen cleaning can be included so dust and buildup do not get between you and the newly cleaned glass.

03

French Panes

Small divided-light panes are cleaned carefully by hand, where consistent detail makes the finished window.

04

Skylights

Interior and exterior skylight glass is evaluated for condition and safe access before it is added to the scope.

05

Tall Interior Glass

Entryways, great rooms and walls of glass receive a deliberate plan that respects the surrounding interior.

06

Difficult Access

Ladder work, sloped ground, landscaping and unusual layouts are assessed before the right cleaning approach is confirmed.

Experience Matters

Complicated glass needs a thoughtful plan.

The hardest part is often everything around the window: height, furniture, finished floors, landscaping, rooflines and the way the window system comes apart.

  • Access evaluated before the work is confirmed
  • Care around floors, furniture and landscaping
  • Extra detail time built into the estimate
  • Clear communication if conditions change on site

Common Questions

Before we tackle the difficult windows.

Do you clean storm windows?

Yes. We clean traditional storm windows and multi-surface window systems. Their configuration and condition are considered when preparing the estimate.

Can you clean French panes and divided-light windows?

Yes. Individual panes and divided-light glass are cleaned carefully by hand, with the extra detail time reflected in the scope.

Do you clean skylights and high interior windows?

Yes, when safe access is available. We evaluate skylights, tall interior glass, ladder-access windows and unusual layouts before confirming the work.

Can screens be cleaned or repaired?

Screen cleaning can be included with window service. Contact us to discuss the condition of damaged screens and the appropriate next step.

How do I get an estimate for difficult windows?

Call or text 330-353-8326 with your address and photos. Photos help us evaluate the type of glass, height and access before the visit.
